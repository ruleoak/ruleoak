# CrashLab

CrashLab is deterministic reliability and chaos testing for actions crossing a RuleOak-controlled boundary. It is not a model-intelligence benchmark.

## v1.1 lifecycle

Faults can occur before authorization, after authorization but before effect, before effect, during a partial effect, after an effect before response, or immediately before response. Synthetic authority may revoke on a later retry. Results include a lifecycle trace, ambiguous-failure count, authority denials, effect certainty and deterministic oracles.

Committed scenarios never target real destructive services.
