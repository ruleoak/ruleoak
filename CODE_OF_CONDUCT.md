# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RuleOak a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences
- giving and accepting constructive feedback
- focusing discussion on technical evidence and user impact
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
- protecting private data and security reports

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- personal or political attacks unrelated to the project
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- disclosing unpatched vulnerabilities through public channels
- knowingly submitting secrets, confidential data, or unsafe test material

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed at https://ruleoak.com/contact.html. Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
